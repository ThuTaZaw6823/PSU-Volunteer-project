01 / OUR PURPOSE

Making English
feel like play.

Learning a language starts with the confidence to try.

English Fun Camp is a PSU student volunteer project for Thai children aged 7–12. Through games, interactive activities and engaging learning materials, we aim to make English enjoyable and build confidence in everyday language use.

The camp focuses on vocabulary, listening, reading and communication, with spelling and teamwork woven into the activities.

03 / LEARNING THROUGH PLAY

Four stations.
Different ways to shine.

Tap a station to see how we learn.
Every game gives English a purpose.

STATION 01Shadow +Look. Guess. Say it.

Look at the shadow of an object, guess what it is and repeat its English name together.

Vocabulary · Observation
STATION 02Pick It Up +Listen. Find. Connect.

Listen to an English word, find the matching object and pick it up. Then say the word together.

Listening · Vocabulary
STATION 03Snake +Think. Build. Team up.

Answer questions to earn snake pieces. Work together to build the longest snake and compare it with the other groups.

Teamwork · Communication
STATION 04Telephone Game +Whisper. Spell. Remember.

Whisper a word and its spelling down the line. Check the last answer against the original word. Also called “Spell” in our teaching plan.

Listening · Spelling · Memory

THE LEARNING GOES BOTH WAYS

We grew, too.

Teaching skills. Confidence. Responsibility.
And a stronger sense of teamwork.

What challenged us

  • Some volunteers lacked confidence speaking English.
  • Keeping a large group of children engaged was challenging.
  • Time was limited, and team coordination needed care.

How we responded

  • Practised useful English phrases before the activities.
  • Stayed calm and communicated with the children.
  • Shared responsibilities, supported each other and adjusted activities to fit the time.
